Do / Don't

Do greyscale UI; colour only for money meaningDon't tinted buttons, coloured nav, rainbow icon tiles
Do depth with hairline + top highlight + soft shadowDon't flat fills with no edge, or heavy drop shadows
Do 6 / 8 / 12 / 16 radii, nested radius = outer − paddingDon't pill-shaped everything or 20+ px card radii
Do Geist, tight tracking on large numbers, tabular figuresDon't proportional or centred numbers
