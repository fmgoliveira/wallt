# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

**Our standard:** be respectful and constructive; assume good faith; give and accept feedback graciously; focus on what
is best for the project and its users. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and publishing others' private
information are not acceptable.

**Enforcement:** maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions, and ban people who violate this standard.
Report problems privately to the maintainer via the contact on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/fmgoliveira).
